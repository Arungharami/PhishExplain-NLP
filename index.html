01 / THE PROBLEM

Three signals.
One critical question.

Can we reliably extract the linguistic evidence of a phishing attempt without mistaking ordinary business messages for attacks?

↗
01 / LINK PATTERNS

Suspicious link cues

Identify URLs and domains inside a message as candidates for review. A URL match alone is never proof of phishing.

02 / PRESSURE LANGUAGE

Urgency & pressure

Detect phrases that push recipients toward rushed decisions through deadlines, warnings, and consequences.

“act now” · “within 24 hours”
◇
03 / SENSITIVE REQUESTS

Credential requests

Surface explicit requests for passwords, verification codes, or account credentials, including their context.

“enter your password”
