What Shield does

  • Reads the message structure and shows observed facts (for example, "this link uses HTTP").
  • Applies documented heuristics (for example, "this domain resembles paypal.com") and labels them as interpretations.
  • Shows external intelligence only when a configured provider actually returns a result, with provider name and timestamp.
  • Keeps an optional AI explanation separate, consent-based and grounded in exact quotes.

What Shield does not do

  • It does not verify SPF, DKIM or DMARC from pasted headers.
  • It does not open links, follow redirects or scan attachments for malware.
  • It does not connect to your inbox, block, quarantine or delete mail.
  • Its attention index is not a probability and a low value never means "safe".
  • Detection accuracy has not yet been measured on a validated benchmark.

Understand the threats

If you think you have already responded to a phishing email, change your password from the official website, enable multi-factor authentication, and contact your bank or IT team. U.S. users can also report phishing at reportfraud.ftc.gov.
