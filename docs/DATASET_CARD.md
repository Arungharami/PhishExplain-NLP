# PhishExplain-NLP dataset card

Data: 60 AI-assisted synthetic candidate email texts; no real private emails, no confidential mail. Source: data/candidate_emails.json. Five balanced scenario families, 12 messages each. Challenge-set families are not gold labels. Initially **all 60 examples are UNREVIEWED**. The split manifest freezes 20 calibration candidates and 40 held-out test candidates; each family contributes 4 and 8 respectively. Author must validate phrasing, label exact UTF-16 character spans and save rationales. No model outputs or gold labels can be inferred from candidate categories. All links use reserved example domains; do not click URLs embedded in arbitrary future inputs. Limitation: synthetic diversity and realism may be poor, and author-generated labels are subjective.
