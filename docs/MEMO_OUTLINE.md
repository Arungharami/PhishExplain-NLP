# CAP 6640 final executive memo outline

Professor Juan Yepes requires a PDF memo of **5–10 pages** (references excluded), worth **60%** of the final project, plus an evidence spreadsheet worth **40%**. Optional third approach: +10% bonus. Final due December 4, 2026.

1. **Problem:** domain, email text and practical motivation.
2. **Literature review:** at least five credible sources actually opened, including three research papers.
3. **Domain technologies:** at least one relevant NLP model or application not covered in class; explain comparative role.
4. **Approaches:** transparent lexical rules versus versioned prompted transformer.
5. **Results:** real precision, recall, F1 or rubric on the same test set, with output evidence.
6. **Error analysis:** three actual explained errors per approach.
7. **Recommendation and risks:** adoption trade-offs, privacy, hallucination, bias and false positives.
8. **Reflection:** about half a page in first person; personal judgment versus AI assistance.
9. **References:** IEEE or APA format, no fabricated sources.

Proposed page allocation: problem .75, literature 1, domain tech .5, methods 1, results 1, errors 1.25, recommendation .75, reflection .5, references separate.
