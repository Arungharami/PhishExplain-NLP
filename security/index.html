02 / BUILT FOR CLARITY

Evidence, not empty confidence.

Every observation has a specific reason. No mysterious “99% safe” badges and no assumptions about links we have not visited.

↗
01 · LINK INTELLIGENCE

Look at the destination

Inspect URL hostnames, HTTP links, IP-address destinations, shortened links, encoded hostnames, and display-text mismatches without opening anything.

Structural signals · No reputation lookup
◈
02 · IDENTITY CLUES

Question sender details

Compare visible From and Reply-To domains and view any pasted SPF, DKIM, or DMARC claims—clearly marked as unverified.

No SMTP or DNS authentication performed
⌁
03 · SOCIAL ENGINEERING

Expose pressure tactics

Highlight urgent language and credential-request phrases. Quoted advice and legitimate messages may produce matches, so context still matters.

Explainable lexical detection · No risk probability
PRIVACY-FIRST BY DESIGN

Keep sensitive decisions
in your hands.

Manual message review runs locally. It does not integrate with your inbox, quarantine mail, scan attachments, look up live domain reputation, or verify authentication records. For a suspected attack, contact the organization using a known trusted channel.
